### 5. Buying and paying

- Prices are in naira. Checkout shows the items, any delivery fee and the total before you pay.
- Payments are processed by Paystack. CampusMart never sees or stores your card details.
- An unpaid order expires after 30 minutes. Until then you can try paying again.
- Once you have paid you can't cancel the order yourself. The seller can cancel before handing it over, and you are refunded (section 7).
- Payments made outside CampusMart aren't protected by the payment hold or refunds.

### 6. Delivery and pickup

For each store in your order you choose how you get it:

- Delivery by a CampusMart rider. On campus, you pick a place from your school's list and pay one flat delivery fee for the whole order, shown at checkout. For an address off campus, the fee depends on distance and is agreed with the rider outside the app. You give a phone number so the rider can reach you.
- Pickup from the seller, where the seller allows it. There is no delivery fee. After you pay you see the seller's address and phone number, and you have 5 days to collect.

Each store has a 3-digit handover code. Give it only when you have your items: entering it confirms the handover. After 5 wrong codes the order locks until CampusMart unlocks it.

The rider must collect from the seller within 3 days of payment. If not, that store's order is cancelled and refunded. If you don't collect from the seller within 5 days, that store's order is cancelled as your fault (section 7).

Riders see the addresses and your phone number only after accepting your delivery. Buyers and sellers see only the rider's name.

### 7. Payment hold, refunds and disputes

CampusMart holds your payment and doesn't pay the seller until you have your items. After the handover you have 48 hours to report a problem from your order. If you don't, the seller is paid.

Before the handover, "Report a problem" tells CampusMart about a late or lost delivery. After the handover, a report opens a dispute: you choose a reason (not as described, damaged, wrong item, missing items, other) and can add up to 3 photos. The seller isn't paid while the dispute is open.

CampusMart decides each dispute: a full refund of that store's order, or payment to the seller. If an item has to come back, CampusMart arranges it with you and the seller. CampusMart also decides lost or damaged parcels case by case.

Refunds depend on whose fault it was:

- The seller's fault (the seller cancels, the store still isn't ready after the rider reported it, or a dispute decided against the seller): a full refund. The seller's trust score goes down.
- Your fault (you didn't collect from the seller in time, or a dispute decided against you): a refund minus that order's share of Paystack's processing fee.
- Nobody's fault (for example, no rider collected in time, or your payment arrived after the order expired and the stock was gone): a full refund.

The delivery fee is refunded only when the whole order is refunded before anything was picked up. Refunds go back through Paystack to the way you paid; your bank decides how long they take to show.

### 8. Seller payouts

- A seller is paid for each store order by bank transfer through Paystack, to the account they saved, once the 48-hour window ends without a dispute.
- After a seller changes their payout account, payouts pause for 24 hours and the seller is told, in case someone else made the change.
- CampusMart doesn't take a platform fee today. Any fee will be added to these Terms before it applies, and taken from the seller's payout.

### 9. Reviews and trust

After the handover, buyers can rate each item once. Reviews must be honest and respectful; CampusMart may hide abusive ones. Sellers with a trust score of 80 or more show a "Trusted seller" badge; cancellations that are the seller's fault lower the score.

### 10. Your conduct, suspension and closing your account

Don't commit fraud, post fake listings or reviews, harass anyone, misuse handover codes, or use another user's details for anything but their order.

CampusMart may suspend an account that breaks these Terms. A suspended user sees the reason and can contact support.

You can delete your account in Account security once you have no open orders or payouts. Your personal details are then removed; order and payment records are kept for 6 years, as tax law requires (see the Privacy Policy).

### 11. Our responsibility

To the extent the law allows, CampusMart isn't liable for indirect losses, and our liability for an order is limited to the amount paid for it. ### 1. Who we are
